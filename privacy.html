Privacy Policy — Roofing Expert

Effective date: August 22, 2026 Publisher: Adam Fischer, independent developer, Florida ("we," "us," "the developer") Applies to: the Roofing Expert mobile application for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch (the "App")

The short version

Roofing Expert stores your inspection reports and photos on your device. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your data, and we do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

The App also includes an optional feature called Company Sync, which lets a company share reports between its own people. Company Sync is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, the reports you publish are stored in your own Apple iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit service, and are visible to the people you invite. They are not sent to us.

Information the App handles — and where it stays

On your device, always. Inspection reports, the photos you capture, measurements, notes, customer name and address, carrier and claim number if you enter them, and your app settings are stored on the device. If you use iCloud Backup, your device’s backup may include them, in the same way it includes other app data. That backup is controlled by you in iOS Settings.

Company Sync — what leaves the device when you turn it on

Company Sync is off by default. Turning it on lets you publish reports to a shared company area so that people you invite can open them.

When you publish a report with Company Sync on, the following is written to your own iCloud account, in a storage container used only by Roofing Expert:

  • The report itself — everything the report contains, including the customer name and property address, carrier and claim number if you entered them, roof measurements, condition findings, and your notes.
  • The photos attached to the report, as image files, along with any annotated versions you created.
  • For each photo: its caption, the date and time it was captured, and — where the photo carries them — the latitude and longitude of the capture location.
  • Review notes left on a report.
  • A company profile and, for each member, the role you assigned them.

This uses Apple’s CloudKit service under your own Apple Account. We have no server, no account system of our own, and no ability to read what is stored there.

Who can see a shared report

A company owner invites people by sending them a share link. Anyone who accepts that invitation can open the reports in that company’s shared area, subject to the role the owner gives them: a read-only member can view but not change reports, and a read-write member can also make changes. Those permissions are enforced by Apple’s sharing system, not only by the App.

Sharing a report shares what is in it — including the customer name and address, and the photo capture locations described above. Please consider that before inviting someone.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, receive, or store your reports, photos, or customer information on any system of ours. We do not operate a server. When Company Sync is on, the App writes to your iCloud account, not to us.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs that profile you.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone.
  • Company Sync does not make your data public. It is shared only with people the company owner invites.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or handle your payment details. Apple tells the App only whether a subscription is currently active.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data is on your device and, if you use Company Sync, in your own iCloud account, you control it:

  • Deleting a report in the App removes it from the device.
  • Removing a member ends that person’s access to the company’s shared reports.
  • Closing a company deletes the company’s shared area from the owner’s iCloud account.
  • Deleting the App removes its on-device data. Data already in iCloud is managed through your Apple Account.

We hold nothing, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

Roofing Expert is a professional tool for roofing inspection and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect your information, there is no data held by us for you to access, correct, export, or have deleted. Your reports and photos are yours, on your device and in your own Apple Account, and the App lets you export them at any time.

Changes to this policy

If the App changes what it does with information, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed with it.
